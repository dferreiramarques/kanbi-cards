What it does

Everything a status board needs — none of the kanban ceremony.

A kamishibai board answers one question: what's still pending? Then it gets out of the way.

Three cycles

Weekly, monthly and annual boards. Cards return to red automatically at the start of each cycle — no "archiving old tasks" chores.

Flip to done

One click flips a card from red to green. Complete a whole cycle and a little confetti celebrates with you.

All cards view

See every cycle at once — weekly, monthly and annual progress side by side, each with its own counter.

Owner colours & filter

Assign a card to a person; each owner gets a colour. Tap the chips above the board to see only that person's cards.

Installs as an app

Add Kanbi Cards to your phone or desktop home screen. Works offline once installed — no store needed.

Free & local

No account, no subscription, no tracking. Boards are stored in your browser's local storage, on your device.

No middleman

Kanbi Cards doesn't keep your data. Your device does.

There's no Kanbi database and no backend — cards are saved in your browser's local storage and nothing ever leaves your machine.

No account required

Open the board and start writing cards. No sign-in, no server, no database of ours — what you type is only ever on your device.

Yours to export

Board data is stored locally in your browser, unencrypted for you. Clear your browser data and it's gone — so back up what matters.

How it works

A status board you can explain in four steps.

Kamishibai is a lean technique from shop floors: cards signal state. Red is pending, green is done.

Open the board

Nothing to install (though you can). Nothing to configure. Just open the app.

Write your cards

Add a card for anything you want to keep visible this week — one card, one task, max 70 characters.

Flip them as you finish

Red card, green card. A glance at the wall tells you exactly what's still pending.

Let the cycle reset

Monday morning, every card flips back to red for the new week. The annual cycle waits for January 1st.
